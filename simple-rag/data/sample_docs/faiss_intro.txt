FAISS is a library developed by Meta AI for efficient similarity search
and clustering of dense vectors. It supports algorithms that search in
sets of vectors of any size, up to ones that possibly do not fit in RAM.
FAISS is written in C++ with complete wrappers for Python. Some of the
most useful algorithms are implemented on the GPU.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation, or RAG, combines a retriever with a
generator. The retriever finds relevant chunks of text from a knowledge
base, usually using vector similarity search, and the generator (an LLM)
uses those chunks as context to produce a grounded answer. This reduces
hallucination compared to asking an LLM a question with no supporting
context, because the model can point to the retrieved passages instead
of relying purely on what it memorized during training.

Groq is known for running open models such as Llama with very low
inference latency, thanks to their custom LPU hardware. This makes it a
popular choice for RAG systems where a fast response matters.
